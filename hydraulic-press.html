Hydraulic Press

Why?
  • The department planned to automate a hydraulic press already available in its lab
  • This involved studying the machine’s design, components and assembly
What?
  • Designed a hydraulic press
  • CAD modelling individual components and assembling them into a complete system
Results?
  • Evaluated the structural integrity of the working bed using FEA, with a maximum von Mises stress of 65 MPa.
  • Maximum deformation was 0.117 mm under the analysed loading conditions.
How?

Studying the hydraulic press, its components and how they fit together.

Modelling individual components, assembling them into a complete system and sing FEA to evaluate the working bed under the applied loading conditions.

The Assembly

Component Breakdown

H-Frame

Provides the main structural support and withstands the loads generated during pressing

Material: Mild steel

Upper Beam

Supports the upper part of the press assembly and transfers loads to the frame

Material: Mild steel

Lower Beam

Supports the workpiece and provides a rigid base for the pressing operation

Material: Mild steel

Piston

Converts hydraulic pressure into linear motion to generate the pressing force

Material: Steel

Beam Base

Provides a flat supporting surface for positioning the workpiece during pressing

Material: Mild steel

Base

Supports and locates the hydraulic mechanism within the press assembly

Material: Mild steel

Work Piece

Represents the component positioned beneath the punch for the pressing operation

Material: Mild steel

Fastener

Secures the assembled components and helps maintain structural alignment

Material: Steel

Punch

Applies concentrated force to the workpiece to perform the intended pressing or forming operation

Material: Hardened tool steel
