Stair Climbing Wheelchair

Why?
  • Conventional wheelchairs are built primarily for flat surfaces and cannot independently navigate staircases.
  • Existing stair-climbing wheelchairs can be expensive and complex, making them inaccessible to many users.
What?
  • Developed the CAD design of a stair-climbing wheelchair using a novel sun–planet gear mechanism for movement across flat surfaces and staircases.
Results?
  • Developed a mechanical concept for transitioning between flat-ground mobility and stair climbing.
  • Focused on a lightweight, cost-effective alternative to conventional stair-climbing wheelchair systems.
How?

Sun–Planet Gear System: Transmits hand-driven power to the rear wheels for movement on flat surfaces.

Lever-Based Mode Switching: Changes the mechanism from driving the wheels to rotating the planet carrier, enabling stair-climbing movement.

Forward–Backward Control: Uses the gear mechanism and lever-based mode selection to adapt movement between flat-ground travel and stair climbing, with a hydraulic disc brake for controlled stopping.

The Assembly

Component Breakdown

Chair

Supports the user and provides the main seating structure of the wheelchair

Material: Mild steel frame

Wheel arrangement

Houses the three-wheel planetary arrangement used to negotiate stair steps

Material: Aluminium alloy for structural arms

Back wheel structure

Supports the rear of the wheelchair and transmits motion during movement

Material: Aluminium alloy

Planet Gear Belt

Transmits motion between the planetary gear mechanism and connected rotating components

Material: Reinforced rubber

Brake Clutch

Engages or disengages the braking mechanism to control wheel movement

Material: Steel

Front Wheel

Supports the front of the wheelchair and enables movement over flat surfaces

Material: Rubber tyre with a aluminium wheel hub

Hand Support

Provides a hand-operated interface for controlling

Material: Steel rod

Disc Brake

Provides braking force to slow down or stop the wheelchair

Material: Stainless steel disc with a suitable friction pad material
