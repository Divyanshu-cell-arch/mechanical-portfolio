Pick and Place
Robotic Arm

Why?
  • The robotics lab had a rotary bottle-filling system, but transferring filled bottles into crates was a separate operation.
  • The project explored automating this step using a robotic arm that could pick filled bottles from the rotary table and place them into a collection crate.
What?
  • Designed and developed a 4-DOF robotic arm to transfer filled bottles from a rotary table into a collection crate.
Results?
  • Completed the mechanical design and assembly of the robotic arm.
  • Moving towards prototype development, with physical testing and performance evaluation planned as the next stage.
How?

Developed 3D CAD models and assembled the robotic arm, gripper, rotary table and collection crate into an integrated system.

Analysed the arm's kinematics, joint arrangement and torque requirements to plan bottle pick-and-place movements.

Defined the sensor placement and motor arrangement for coordinating bottle detection, picking and placement.

The Assembly

Component Breakdown

Ground Link

The main structure supporting the press assembly

Material: Aluminium alloy

Base to Stepper

Connects the base structure to the stepper motor and helps transmit rotational motion to the arm.

Material: Aluminium alloy

Connector

Connects adjacent structural components and maintains their alignment during arm movement.

Material: Aluminium alloy

Bridge

Provides a rigid mounting interface between connected arm components and helps transfer mechanical loads.

Material: Aluminium alloy

Upper Arm

Forms the main structural link between joints and extending the arm's reach

Material: Aluminum alloy

Mid Arm

Connects the upper and forearm sections, enabling the arm to position the gripper over the target.

Material: Aluminium alloy

Fore Arm

Connects the mid-arm section to the gripper assembly and positions it near the bottle.

Material: Aluminium alloy

Gear

Transfers rotational motion between meshing gears to drive the arm's joint mechanism.

Material: Steel

Lead Screw Horn

Provides a mechanical mounting for lead screw

Material: Steel

DS3240 Horn

Provides a mechanical mounting for DS3240 Servo motor

Material: Aluminium alloy

MG 996R Horn

Provides a mechanical mounting for MG996 Servo motor

Material: Aluminium alloy

Gripper

Grasps and holds bottles securely during transfer from the rotary table to the collection crate.

Material: Aluminium alloy

Stepper Motor with Lead Screw

Provide transaltional motion

Material: Aluminium alloy casing

DS3240 Servomotor

Provides rotational motion

Material: Aluminium alloy casing

MG 996R DC Motor

Provides rotational motion

Material: Aluminium alloy casing
